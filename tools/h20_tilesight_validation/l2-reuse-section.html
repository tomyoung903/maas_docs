6b. Inspect A and B reuse at every K step

Each of the 32 × 32 output tiles has 64 K steps. Move the K slider or select a cell to inspect its A and B loads. The left half of each cell is A; the right half is B. Colors show the modeled probability of an L2 hit, and a cross marks a compulsory first read from HBM. The view combines all 14 modeled waves; it is not a snapshot of simultaneous execution.

Full-access calculation, not measured cache counters. This explorer examines all 65,536 A loads and all 65,536 B loads without event sampling. Its mean L2-hit estimates are 96.71% for A and 91.70% for B. L2 capacity is 60 MiB, shared across the GPU. One A or B tile occupies 16 KiB, so that is the capacity equivalent of 3,840 input tiles.

The historical timing calculation above used the fast sampled cache model. A later audit found that its 324.54-byte A estimate undercounts compulsory reads: a cold, uncompressed A matrix requires at least 512 HBM bytes per load on average. The full-access calculation gives 538.72 bytes for A and 1,359.58 bytes for B. Those updated cache estimates are used in this explorer; the original timing results above are retained as recorded.
