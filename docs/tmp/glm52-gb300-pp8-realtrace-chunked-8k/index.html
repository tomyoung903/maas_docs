5. Data, related reports, and measurement semantics

Cadence is the central-80 mean reconstructed from PP0 prefill log intervals. TPM is uncached prompt tokens divided by client wall time. Internal TTFT is prefill_finished_time − forward_entry_time; the 13,019,893-byte server-log segment contained 2,048 unique request-finished records and zero malformed records. The cache was flushed before every replay; natural prefix reuse then emerged from the original ordered request stream.
