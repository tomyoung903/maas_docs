        What MFU and MBUmin Mean Here

        Both percentages are static-model readings over traced active-kernel interval union. MBUmin is a useful-byte floor, not a hardware-counter measurement of physical HBM traffic.

        FeqFP8 work plus twice the BF16 MLA work Dminoperation-specific useful-byte lower bound t∪union of this row's traced GPU 0 kernel intervals PFP81,979 TFLOP/s dense H200 FP8 reference BHBM4.8 TB/s H200 HBM reference
        MFU=Feq ÷ (t∪ × PFP8) × 100%
        MBUmin=Dmin ÷ (t∪ × BHBM) × 100%
        Do not over-read a low number. A low MFU can be expected for small or irregular GEMMs, routing, and launch-heavy support work. A low MBUmin only says the conservative useful-byte floor is small relative to peak HBM; actual DRAM traffic and cache behavior require Nsight Compute counters.
